PocketLocket Privacy Policy

Effective July 20, 2026

PocketLocket is a local-first personal budgeting app. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Information stored on your device

PocketLocket stores account names, balances, transactions, categories, rules, preferences, and optional budgets in its local SwiftData store on your device.

If you connect SimpleFIN, the app stores its read-only access URL in the device Keychain, protected by Face ID, Touch ID, or your passcode when available.

If you add PocketLocket's Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, the app writes a summary of your current month into a private app-group container that only PocketLocket and its widget can read. The widget makes no network requests, and amounts stay hidden unless you turn them on in Settings.

Optional SimpleFIN connection

SimpleFIN is optional. When you choose to connect it, the app communicates directly with SimpleFIN over HTTPS to retrieve the accounts and transactions you authorize. PocketLocket does not operate a backend or receive a copy of that data.

SimpleFIN privacy information

Privacy protections

PocketLocket does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not sell your information or create an app account for you.

Face ID and Touch ID are handled by Apple. PocketLocket receives only the authentication result, never biometric data.

On supported devices, you can ask Apple Intelligence questions about recent transactions. Questions and transaction context are processed on-device and are not sent to a server operated by the developer.

Backups and control

Encrypted backups are created only when you request them. The app saves them to the location you choose, and the backup password is not retained by the app.

Use Settings > Reset local data to remove PocketLocket's local accounts, transactions, category rules, merchant-grouping corrections, and reimbursement allocations from this device, choosing whether to keep or remove the SimpleFIN connection. You can also revoke SimpleFIN access through SimpleFIN.
